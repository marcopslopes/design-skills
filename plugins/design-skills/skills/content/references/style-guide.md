## Brand values
- **Time back** – every screen should save the provider a step, not add one
- **Regulars first** – the clients a provider already has come before new ones
- **Plain money** – amounts, fees and payout dates are always visible and never hidden in small print

## Voice and tone

### Core voice (both apps)
Calm, specific, on your side.

### Northline Studio (provider app)
Goal: providers feel the app is a capable colleague that handles the admin.

### Northline (client app)
Goal: clients can book, move or pay in a few taps and trust what they see.

## Writing essentials
- Write about the reader's job, not about Northline's features
- If cutting words makes something harder to understand, keep the words
- Active voice by default; passive is fine to soften an error ("Your card wasn't charged")
- Gender-neutral, no slang or idioms that won't travel
- No emoji in product copy

---

## Grammar

### Contractions
Use them ("you're", "it's", "can't").

### Numbers
Numerals everywhere (2, 15, 40), except at the start of a sentence.

### Continue vs. Next
Use "Continue" on multi-step flows.

---

## Punctuation

| Mark | Rule |
|------|------|
| Ampersand (&) | Only in brand names or tight tab labels |
| Dashes | Use a spaced en dash (–) for asides |
| Ellipsis (…) | Only for in-progress states ("Sending…") |
| Exclamation mark (!) | Real milestones only; never in errors |
| Oxford comma | Yes, in lists of 3 or more |

---

## Error messages
- Say what happened, in plain words
- Say what to do next
- Never blame the reader; never show error codes alone

| ✓ Do | ✗ Don't |
|------|---------|
| We couldn't take the deposit. Ask Ana to check her card, or book without one. | Transaction failed (402). |
