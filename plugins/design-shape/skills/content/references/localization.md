## How to use this guide

1. Find the feature name in the table below.
2. Use the EN term for all English-language markets.
3. For other markets, note the approved translated term and add it to your "Needs review" section if you're unsure.
4. If a term isn't in the table, flag it so it can be added before shipping — never translate a product name yourself.

## Notes on markets

**Brand names stay in English.** "Northline Holds" and "Spotlight" are never translated; descriptive features ("Deposits", "Rebook") are.

**Regional variants:** if your product serves both Spain and Latin America (or France and Quebec), keep separate columns — terms often differ.

**Currency, date and time formats:** follow your org's localization UX guide. Don't guess market-specific formats.
