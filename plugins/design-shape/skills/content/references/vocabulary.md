# Northline Vocabulary Guidelines – Reference (example pack)

Capitalize product and feature names (e.g. Northline Holds, Bulletins). Use lowercase for generic descriptions of what something does (e.g. "taking a deposit", "sending a reminder").

---

## A–B

**Order** — What a trade customer places. Not "transaction" or "ticket".

**Bulletins** — A message sent to a group of trade customers. Title case. Not "blasts", "campaigns" or "mass messages".

**store** — The business using Northline. Not "merchant" or "vendor" in product copy.

## C–D

**trade customers** — Always use for a store's regular buyers. Never "users" or "consumers" in product copy.

**Deposits** — Money taken on a special order. Not "prepayments" or "down payments".

## E–R

**Margin** — The store's money views. Not "revenue", "reports" or "analytics" in UI copy.

**Northline Holds** — The deposit policy for special orders. Title case, full name on first use, "Holds" after that. Never "order protection" as a product name.

**Northline Counter / Northline Trade** — The store app and the customer app. Use exactly as written.

**Shelf cards** — Printable labels and signs for the shop floor. Not "flyers" or "posts".

**Reorder** — Ordering a regular line again. One word, no hyphen.

## S–Z

**Featured** — Optional paid placement for a supplier's line. A noun, never a verb ("Turn on Featured", not "Feature your line").
