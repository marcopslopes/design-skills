Capitalize product and feature names (e.g. Fernwell Holds, Broadcasts). Use lowercase for generic descriptions of what something does (e.g. "taking a deposit", "sending a reminder").

---

## A–B

**Appointment / booking** — **Client-facing copy:** prefer "appointment". **Provider-facing copy (Fernwell Studio):** prefer "booking" — providers run a book of bookings.

**Broadcasts** — A message sent to a group of clients. Title case. Not "blasts", "campaigns" or "mass messages".

**business** — Use instead of "salon". Many Fernwell providers work from a studio, a room at home, or a client's home.

## C–D

**clients** — Always use for a provider's end-users. Never "customers", "users" or "consumers" in product copy.

**Deposits** — Money taken at booking. Not "prepayments" or "down payments".

## E–R

**Earnings** — The provider's money views. Not "revenue", "reports" or "analytics" in UI copy.

**Fernwell Holds** — The deposit and late-cancel policy feature. Title case, full name on first use, "Holds" after that. Never "no-show protection" as a product name.

**Fernwell Studio / Fernwell** — The provider app and the client app. Use exactly as written.

**Promo Cards** — Shareable images for a provider's social channels. Not "flyers" or "posts".

**Rebook** — Booking the next visit. One word, no hyphen.

## S–Z

**Spotlight** — Optional paid placement. A noun, never a verb ("Turn on Spotlight", not "Spotlight your profile").
